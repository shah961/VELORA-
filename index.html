Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes. All domestic orders are fulfilled via courier Cash on Delivery (COD). You simply inspect the package and pay the courier upon delivery.

How can I place an order?

Select your perfume, click 'Order Now', fill in your delivery details, and click 'Send Order Details to WhatsApp'. Our concierges instantly receive your pre-formatted order summary.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes between 2 to 4 business days depending on your target city. Express dispatch is executed within 24 hours of order confirmation.

Can I order multiple perfumes in one order?

Yes. You can add distinct products to your bag or specify additional quantities and fragrances within the checkout notes form.

How can I contact customer support?

Direct WhatsApp messaging is active 24/7 for order updates, fragrance recommendations, and custom gifting inquiries.
