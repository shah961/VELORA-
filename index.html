OUR PHILOSOPHY

Fragrance is not simply something you wear. It is how you are remembered.

VELORAÉ was created around a singular, uncompromising vision: to revive the lost art of slow, high-concentration perfumery. Each blend requires up to 180 days of maturation in dark oak barrels.

We source our Rose de Mai from Grasse, wild Oud from Cambodia, and Bergamot from Calabria. The result is an invisible aura of quiet luxury and distinction.

INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

You place your order through our website checkout, which formats your request and sends it straight to our WhatsApp sales line. Once confirmed, we dispatch your bottle. You pay cash directly to the courier upon delivery.

All our creations are Extrait de Parfum concentration (30% pure fragrance oils). This guarantees superior sillage and endurance exceeding 14 hours on skin.

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours. Express delivery typically arrives within 2 to 4 business days depending on your city location.

Every full bottle order includes a complimentary 2ml discovery vial of the same scent. You may test the vial first; if unsuited, return the sealed full bottle effortlessly.
